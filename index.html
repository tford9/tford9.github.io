Trenton W. Ford

Assistant Professor of Data Science. I study how AI reshapes society — and advise the teams building it.

I direct the Ford AI & Society (FAIS) Lab at William & Mary, working on computational disinformation, network science, and human–digital twins. I lead two DARPA initiatives on human–AI teaming and large-language-model evaluation, and I advise organizations putting AI to work responsibly.
