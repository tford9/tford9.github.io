Our paper **DAVIS: Planning Agent with Knowledge Graph-Powered Inner Monologue** was accepted to EMNLP 2025 (Findings).

DAVIS builds on our ReasonPlanner work, advancing the state of autonomous planning agents that can reason about dynamic environments. The system uses knowledge graph structures to power an inner monologue capability, enabling more coherent and context-aware planning.

Co-authors: Minh Pham Dinh, Michael G. Yankoski, Munira Syed

[EMNLP 2025 Proceedings](https://aclanthology.org/)
